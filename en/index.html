About

“Yume” means dream in Japanese. One hour that is only yours, between work and home.

Quiet rather than flashy. A clean room, and care shaped to your hands and skin. We speak English, Japanese and Vietnamese.

  • CleanTools are sterilised after every guest. Fresh towels for each visit.
  • UnhurriedAppointments have room to breathe; the finish matters more than the clock.
  • NaturalSubtle, office-friendly looks are a speciality.

FAQ

Yes. We speak English and Vietnamese.

Can I come just for gel removal?

Yes, including gel done at another salon.

I'm pregnant or have a medical condition.

Please tell us when booking. Some services may not be suitable; check with your doctor first.
