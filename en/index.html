FAQ

FAQ

Can I walk in without booking?

If there is space, yes, but the salon is small, so booking is recommended.

I don't speak Japanese. Is that OK?

Yes. We speak Vietnamese, and help in English with a translation tool.

Can I come just for gel removal?

Yes, including gel done at another salon.

I'm pregnant or have a medical condition.

Please tell us when booking. Some services may not be suitable; check with your doctor first.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit card (VISA, Mastercard, JCB, AMEX), PayPay or au PAY.
